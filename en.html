Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions

How do I request a dream interpretation?
Contact us via WhatsApp or phone after Fajr (Cairo time) with your dream and payment details.
What payment methods are accepted?
Al-Kuraimi (Yemeni Rial, Saudi Riyal, US Dollar) and InstaPay (Egyptian Pound).
Is my dream interpretation kept private?
Yes, we maintain complete confidentiality. All dreams stay between the interpreter and the dreamer only.
When can I contact you?
After Fajr prayer (Cairo local time) daily. Interpretation is provided only after payment.
Can I share the interpretation with others?
Yes, but you must cite the source (Roa'a). Sharing without attribution is not permitted.
What are the basics of requesting dream interpretation?
Do not share your dream with anyone, write it down immediately after waking, and contact us after Fajr.
